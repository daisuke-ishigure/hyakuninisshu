Waka Techniques

Makurakotoba

Makurakotoba ("pillow words") like "ashibiki no" or "hisakata no" are fixed five-syllable phrases that lead into a specific word. They set the rhythm of a line and give it a more memorable, evocative quality.

Kakekotoba & Engo

Kakekotoba are homophones with two meanings; engo are words linked by association. Both are techniques that layer multiple meanings into a single poem, enriching its expression.

Jokotoba

Jokotoba are introductory phrases used to lead into a particular word. Made up of seven or more syllables, they were often composed uniquely by each poet.
