Hyakunin Isshu Relationship Diagram

This diagram arranges the poets by the era in which they were active, connecting them with lines to show their relationships. TapClick a poet to see an excerpt from their poem. Double-tapDouble-click a poet you're curious about to narrow the view down to only the poets connected to them (to return to the full view, double-tapdouble-click an empty area).

Drag nodes left or right to adjust their position, or drag a relationship's curve handle to reshape it. Click "Save to file" and, the first time, choose js/network-data_en.json to overwrite it directly (Chrome/Edge only — later clicks reuse the same file without asking again). Other browsers will download the JSON instead; replace js/network-data_en.json with it manually.
