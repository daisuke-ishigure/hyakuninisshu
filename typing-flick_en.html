Hyakunin Isshu — Memorize by Flick Typing

  • Mode A: The upper verse is shown. Type the lower verse.
  • Mode B: The lower verse is shown. Type the kimariji.
  • Mode C: The lower verse is shown. Type the upper verse.
  • Practice with just the 20 poems from a single color of the Five-Color Hyakunin Isshu set (blue, pink, yellow, green, orange).
  • Typing follows modern kana usage (gendai kana-zukai).
  • When you finish typing, your time and KPM (characters typed per minute) are shown. Both are measured from the moment you start typing until you finish — time spent just looking at the question isn't counted.
  • For the kimariji mode only, the timer starts the instant the question appears, and KPM is calculated from that.
  • A higher KPM means faster, smoother typing. Poems you know well tend to score higher, so a low KPM is a good way to spot the cards you still need to practice.
  • For PC (keyboard) input, try this version instead.
