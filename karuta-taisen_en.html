Hyakunin Isshu Competitive Karuta Game

※Reader: Noriko Makifuji

Battling someone online

  • You can play against other players online if any are available.
  • Up to 4 players can compete in real time.
  • If you touch the wrong card (false start), you cannot take any action for 3 seconds.
  • You can automatically join a game in progress until the 3rd card is read (early stages).
No one waiting right now

Battling a Robot

  • You can battle a robot at any time.
  • The first to take 5 cards wins.
  • To heighten immersion, the screen shakes when you swipe a card away. If the shaking makes you feel unwell, select "Off" under "Screen shake when swiping a card".

※ Ranking: a victory counts as 1 win

When on, 1–2 poems with no card on the field (dead cards) are mixed into the reading. Touching any card when a dead card is read is a false start.

Playing with friends

  • Enter an agreed-upon "passphrase" to battle your friends.
  • Passphrases can be Japanese (hiragana, katakana, kanji), alphanumeric, or a combination.

Ranking

  • Log in with a Google account and win in "Battle Someone (Online Battle)" or "Battle a Robot (Robot Battle)" to have your wins reflected in real time on Wins Leaderboard. The ranking resets daily at midnight (Japan Time).
  • In Robot Battle, the number of wins recorded for a victory depends on the level: Easy and Normal count as 1 win, Hard as 2, and Oni as 5. Easy victories are recorded up to 3 times per day.
  • When you sign in, we receive your Google account's display name, email address, and profile photo URL. This information is stored by Firebase (Google) Authentication to verify your sign-in, but it is not shown on the leaderboard or saved in the ranking data (the leaderboard shows the nickname you set). It will not be shared with third parties except as required by law. Please see the Privacy Policy for details.
  • Once you sign in, a "Delete Data" button appears next to "Log Out." It deletes all your ranking data and your sign-in information (your Google account email address, display name, etc.). This cannot be undone.
  • You can play without signing in, but your wins won't be recorded in the ranking.
  • A title badge is displayed next to your name based on the number of wins. Click "🎖 Title List" below to see the details of each title badge.
