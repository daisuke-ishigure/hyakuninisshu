Hyakunin Isshu Competitive Karuta Game

※Reader: Noriko Makifuji

Battling someone online

  • You can play against other players online if any are available.
  • Up to 4 players can compete in real time.
  • If you touch the wrong card (false start), you cannot take any action for 3 seconds.
  • You can automatically join a game in progress until the 3rd card is read (early stages).
No one waiting right now

Battling a Robot

  • You can battle a robot at any time.

※ Easy mode does not count toward the ranking

Playing with friends

  • Enter an agreed-upon "passphrase" to battle your friends.
  • Passphrases can be Japanese (hiragana, katakana, kanji), alphanumeric, or a combination.

Ranking

  • Log in with a Google account and win in "Battle Someone (Online Battle)" or "Battle a Robot (Robot Battle)" (Normal or Hard) to have your wins reflected in real time on Wins Leaderboard. The ranking resets daily at midnight (Japan Time).
  • When you log in, we retrieve your Google account's display name, email address, and profile picture URL. This information is used solely for ranking display purposes and will not be provided to third parties except as required by law. Please see the Privacy Policy for details.
  • If you log in, a "Delete Data" button will appear next to the "Log Out" button. Pressing the "Delete Data" button will delete the data registered in the ranking. However, please note that once deleted, it cannot be restored.
  • You can enjoy the game without logging in, but in that case your wins will not be recorded in the ranking.
  • A title badge is displayed next to your name based on the number of wins. Click "🎖 Title List" below to see the details of each title badge.
