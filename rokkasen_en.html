The Rokkasen (Six Poetic Sages)

In the Kana Jo, the preface to the Kokin Wakashū written in kana, Ki no Tsurayuki picked out six poets as "people whose names were known in recent times" (famous poets of the generation before his) and judged their poetry. All of his judgments are harsh, yet these six poets later came to be called the Rokkasen, the Six Poetic Sages, and were deeply admired.

High Priest Henjō

僧正遍昭

天つ風
雲のかよひ路
吹きとぢよ
をとめの姿
しばしとどめむ

In Japanese

歌のさまは得たれども、まこと少なし。例えば、絵に描ける女を見て、いたづらに心を動かすがごとし。

In English

He has the style of poetry, but little true feeling. It is like being moved, to no purpose, by a woman in a painting.

See High Priest Henjō's page.

Ariwara no Narihira

在原業平

ちはやぶる
神代もきかず
竜田川
から紅に
水くくるとは

In Japanese

その心余りて詞たらず。しぼめる花の色なくて、匂ひ残れるがごとし。

In English

His feelings overflow, but his words fall short. It is like a withered flower that has lost its color but still keeps its scent.

See Ariwara no Narihira's page.

Fun'ya no Yasuhide

文屋康秀

吹くからに
秋の草木の
しをるれば
むべ山風を
嵐といふらむ

In Japanese

詞はたくみにてその様身に負はず。いはば、商人のよき衣着たらむがごとし。

In English

He uses words skillfully, but his poems do not match his feelings. It is like a merchant dressed in fine clothes.

See Fun'ya no Yasuhide's page.

Priest Kisen

喜撰法師

わが庵は
都の辰巳
しかぞすむ
世をうぢ山と
人はいふなり

In Japanese

詞かすかにして、始め終はり確かならず。いはば、秋の月を見るに、暁の雲にあへるがごとし。

In English

His words are vague, and the beginning and end of his poems do not hold together. It is like watching the autumn moon and having it hidden by the clouds of dawn.

See Priest Kisen's page.

Ono no Komachi

小野小町

花の色は
うつりにけりな
いたづらに
わが身よにふる
ながめせしまに

In Japanese

いにしへの衣通姫そとおりひめの流なり。あはれなるやうにて強からず。いはば、よき女のなやめるところあるに似たり。

In English

She follows the style of Princess Sotoori of long ago. Her poems are deeply moving, but they lack strength. It is like a beautiful woman who is unwell.

See Ono no Komachi's page.

Ōtomo no Kuronushi

大友黒主

春さめの
ふるは涙か
桜花
散るを惜しまぬ
人しなければ

In Japanese

そのさまいやし。いはば、薪負へる山人の、花の陰に休めるがごとし。

In English

His style is crude. It is like a mountain woodcutter with firewood on his back, resting in the shade of blossoms.

Of the Six Poetic Sages, only Ōtomo no Kuronushi was left out of the Hyakunin Isshu. It feels a little sad for him.
