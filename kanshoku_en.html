Heian Court Ranks (Ikai) and Offices (Kanshoku)

The Heian period operated under a deeply intricate system of court ranks and official posts. This series covers the central and local government structures based on the ritsuryō codes, the organisation of ladies-in-waiting in the inner palace, the warriors and Kebiishi who maintained order, the Buddhist clergy system, and much more. Understanding the roles and titles held by the Hyakunin Isshu poets brings the background of their poems into sharp focus.
