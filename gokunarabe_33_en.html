Hyakunin Isshu: Meet the Poets
Ki no Tomonori

Meaning

In the peaceful light of a spring day, why do the cherry blossoms scatter so restlessly?

Commentary

A headnote in the Tomonori-shū says the poem was written for "the Empress's poetry contest in the Kanpyō era," so it is thought to have been composed on the theme "falling blossoms." On a calm spring day with no wind, the cherry blossoms flutter down; the poet speaks of them as if they were people in a hurry to fall.

Ki no Tomonori was one of the compilers of the Kokin Wakashū. The poem shows a gentle sensitivity that cares not only for blossoms in full bloom but also for their fleeting fall, and sees in an everyday spring scene the passing of life.
