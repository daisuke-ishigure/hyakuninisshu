Kaminoku Dango

  • The lower verse is shown as the prompt. Select the poem's upper verse (three phrases) and skewer them.
  • Move the skewer left and right — skewering the phrases in the poem's correct order turns them into dango.
  • If you skewer the wrong phrase, the dango already on the skewer will disappear.
  • Complete 5 skewers to clear the game.
  • In "Easy" mode, the first character of the next phrase to skewer is shown as a hint.

Controls:

← → Move the skewer  Space / Click to skewer
Tilt phone to move skewer  Tap to skewer
