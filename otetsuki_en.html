False Start Rules

  • Touching a card in the same field as the correct card → Safe (the card you touch does not need to be the correct one)
  • Touching a card in the wrong field from the correct card → False Start
  • Touching any card when an empty card (not in either field) is read → False Start
  • If you commit a false start, your opponent sends you one card, increasing your field.
  • If your opponent commits a false start, you send one card to them, increasing their field.
  • The card sent as a penalty is called a "送り札" (sent card).
  • The player sending the card may choose whichever card they wish.
